EDITH privacy policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026.

EDITH is a personal assistant used only by its owner on their own computer. It has no other users.

Google data EDITH uses

  • Gmail: reads message headers and text to tell the owner about new mail, and saves drafts. EDITH never sends email on its own.
  • Google Calendar: reads events to tell the owner about their day; creates, moves or deletes an event only after the owner approves that specific change.
  • Google Drive: opens files the owner asks for.

Where the data goes

  • Google data is processed on the owner's own PC. Sign-in tokens are stored there, encrypted with Windows' per-user protection.
  • EDITH does not sell, share or transfer Google user data to anyone, does not use it for advertising, and does not use it to train AI models.
  • To answer a spoken request, the request text may be sent to the language-model service the owner chose. Raw Google data (email bodies, calendar contents) is not sent there unless the owner asks a question about it.

Removing access

The owner can revoke EDITH's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions and delete the local token file.
